PV candidate survey: diatomic molecules and ions

Rotational and hyperfine interval matching for nuclear-spin-dependent parity-violation (NSD-PV) measurements, one row per isotopologue. hf = (I+½)|bF| is the hyperfine interval, and gap = 2B(N+1) − hf is the matching gap from the contact term alone, at the N where the two intervals come closest (any N). δmin is the survey's exact-level matching gap for N ≤ 5. Constants for “as-if-Σ” rows assume the unpaired electron occupies a σ orbital. The DFT columns give the energies (cm⁻¹) of the other states DFT found, relative to the DFT state; they are not a ground-state assignment. Ground states in grey italics with ? are estimates by analogy with another molecule, not from a source on this one.
