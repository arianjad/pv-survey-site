PV candidate survey: diatomic molecules and ions

We survey diatomic molecules and singly charged ions for ground-state structure suited to nuclear-spin-dependent parity-violation (NSD-PV) measurements, where a rotational interval 2B(N+1) nearly matches the hyperfine interval hf = (I+½)|bF|. Each row is one isotopologue. The gap 2B(N+1) − hf uses the contact term alone, at the N where the two intervals come closest (any N). δmin is the smallest zero-field gap, over N ≤ 5, between opposite-parity levels of equal F; it uses exact levels where c or γ is known (or S ≥ 1) and the contact term otherwise. "As-if-Σ" rows are molecules whose ground state is unknown; their constants assume the unpaired electron occupies a σ orbital. The DFT columns give the energies (cm⁻¹) of the other states our DFT calculations found, relative to the DFT state; they are not a ground-state assignment. Ground states in grey italics with ? are estimates by analogy with another molecule, not from a source on this one.

Methods

Constants. Each B and bF is labelled by its source: measured; scaled from a measured or a calculated isotopologue of the same molecule; calculated in the literature; our DFT; or our estimate. Scaling between isotopologues (reduced mass for B, nuclear g-factor for bF) is accurate to the parent value, so a value scaled from a calculation counts as a calculation. Our estimates are either rescaled from another molecule of the same charge (approximate) or zero-order, from atomic radii or the free-ion contact interaction (crude). When several values exist, we use the most direct one, measured first and estimated last.

DFT. We use ORCA 6.1.1 with all-electron scalar-relativistic Hamiltonians. An unrestricted Kohn–Sham scan of the bond length gives re and Be, and at re an X2C calculation (decontracted x2c-TZVPall-s basis, finite nucleus, picture-change corrected) gives bF and c. A DKH2 g-tensor calculation gives Δg⊥, and hence γ = −2BΔg⊥ (Curl relation). We chose the functional for each class of molecule (B3LYP, PBE0 with 40% exact exchange, or BP86) by benchmarking against measured molecules, and the bF uncertainty is the RMS error of that class. These basis sets stop at Rn, so Ra, Ac and Th compounds have no DFT values.

Estimates. Without a measured or calculated value, we estimate the bond length from related molecules of the same charge, scaled by covalent radii (Pyykkö–Atsumi), or from atomic radii alone when no related molecule is known, and bF from the Fermi contact interaction of a related molecule or the free ion, scaled to the target nucleus. The uncertainties come from leave-one-out tests on molecules with known values; estimates are placeholders until a measurement or calculation exists.

Levels and crossings. For 2Σ molecules with enough known constants, we diagonalize the effective Hamiltonian (case (b) basis |N G F MF⟩, with γ, bF, c and eQq) in magnetic fields up to 200 mT. The level plots and crossings show same-MF, opposite-parity level pairs whose PV matrix element |⟨a|n·(I×S)|b⟩|/I exceeds 0.02.
